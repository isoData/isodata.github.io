Stage 4: The Conclusion (The Reveal) 🚨

It wasn't business, detective. It was a crime of pure **passion and possessiveness**.

The Killer: Mickey 🤫

Evidence Breakdown

  • The Weapon: Mickey was known to carry a small, *quiet* gun 🤫, matching the single, silenced shot.
  • The Silver: The broken **silver earring** 👂 Thorne was clutching was Clara's. It was personal.
  • The Mark: The hidden **stylized 'J' mark** 🔥 was Mickey's personal brand of ownership—he left it in Thorne's safe to mark what he considered his.
  • The Entry: Mickey got the key from a nervous Clara 🗝️. No forced entry needed.
Thorne died because he took a girl and a cheap silver necklace from the wrong man. Case closed. 🔒
